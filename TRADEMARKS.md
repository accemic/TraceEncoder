## Use of the RISC-V mark

Accemic Technologies GmbH is **not** a member of RISC-V International. The
mark therefore appears in this repository **only** in its descriptive sense —
naming the architecture and the specifications this encoder targets, as in
*"trace encoder for RISC-V cores"* or *"implements the RISC-V N-Trace
specification"*.

Specifically, this repository does not and must not:

- use the RISC-V logo;
- use "RISC-V" as part of the product, module or domain name — the product is
  **CEDARtools.TraceEncoder**, and RISC-V only describes what it targets;
- claim membership, partnership, certification, approval or endorsement;
- claim conformance or compatibility certified by any official RISC-V
  programme. Deviations from the specifications are listed openly in
  [`doc/trace-format.adoc`](doc/trace-format.adoc), and the vendor TCODEs
  56/57/58 are Accemic extensions that imply no claim of standard conformance.

"RISC-V" is written in block letters with a hyphen; the only admissible
spellings are *RISC-V* and *RISC-V International*.
